New · Group orders

One table. One order. Everyone pays their share.

Foodly's group orders end the awkward bill-splitting moment. Each guest adds their items, sees a transparent breakdown, and pays their own share — securely, from their own phone.

1

Start a group order

Anyone at the table opens the restaurant's menu in Foodly and starts a shared order. Friends join in a tap.

2

Everyone adds their items

Each guest picks their own dishes and drinks. Share plates, cover a friend, split evenly or pay item by item — your table, your rules.

3

Pay your share, done

Everyone pays their own part with a full breakdown shown before confirming — tip included if you like. The restaurant gets paid instantly.

Payments are processed securely by Stripe. Every diner sees the exact amount — including the payment platform's small €0.25 processing fee — before paying. No surprises, for anyone.
